    <div class="kicker">Teaching philosophy</div>
    <h2>Teach the full stack: theory, physics, computation, and evaluation</h2>
    <p>
      My teaching aims to help students connect mathematical principles, scientific modeling, algorithm design, and empirical evaluation. In imaging and machine learning, students benefit most when they learn not just how to run a model, but how the data were acquired, what assumptions are built into the algorithm, and how to judge whether the output is scientifically meaningful.
    </p>
