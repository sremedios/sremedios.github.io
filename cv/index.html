Summary

  • Machine learning researcher working on generative modeling and inverse problems, with a focus on generation under physical constraints and in data-scarce regimes. My methods pair data-driven generative priors with hard consistency to known physics — developed and validated on medical imaging, but built on problem structure that transfers across scientific domains. I build the high-performance training and tooling around my models rather than handing off prototypes.
